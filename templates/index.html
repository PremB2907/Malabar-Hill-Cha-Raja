Historic Heritage & Legacy

The Mandal, in brief

Established in 1973, Shree Bal Gopal Ganeshutsav Mandal has continued a grand public Ganeshotsav tradition through the dedication of local residents, youth volunteers, and senior mentors.

Over the decades, Malabar Hill Cha Raja became an iconic divine symbol cherished by millions of devotees.

More about the mandal

The mandal brings together devotion, Maharashtrian culture, and year-round community service. Its annual celebrations are known for the distinctive idol, thoughtful mandap design, and warm welcome extended to every devotee.

From Annadan Mahaprasad and health initiatives to cultural programs, the work continues through the shared effort of residents, volunteers, and the committee.
